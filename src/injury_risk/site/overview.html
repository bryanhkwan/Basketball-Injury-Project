When and who

What makes an injury more likely?

Each player is compared with their own games in the same month, so it’s the situation, not the player. A pattern only counts if it showed up in both halves of the seven seasons.

  • Injury history and heavy minutes matter most
  • Rest helps; the late season hurts
  • Back-to-backs and travel made no clear difference

How it’s built

From $reports PDFs to a nightly ranking

  1. $reportsRead

    Official NBA injury reports, one for every game day from December 2018 to June 2026.

  2. $linesMatch

    Report lines tied to players and games; $matched matched. Box scores, schedules and play-by-play joined in.

  3. $player_gamesModel

    Player-games scored on injury history, workload, schedule and age with gradient-boosted trees.

  4. $aucTest

    AUC on 2024-25 and 2025-26, seasons the model never saw. The misses are left in.

What it can’t tell you

  • It ranks risk; it doesn’t diagnose it, and it isn’t medical advice.
  • Injury reports say what hurt, not how it happened.
  • It has no practice load, wearables or medical records, which is the data teams have and the public doesn’t.
