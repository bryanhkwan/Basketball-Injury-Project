From reports
to a ranking

Every game day the league publishes an injury report: who is out, and why. I matched every line to a player and a game, then asked one question before every game, for every player: how likely is a new injury in the next five games?

Who made this

$author built this to find out what public data can say about NBA injuries before they happen, and what it can’t.

Everything here comes from public sources: the league’s official injury reports, box scores and play-by-play. The model was tested on two seasons it never saw, misses included. On an average night about $out_per_night players per team sit out with a new injury, and most of those injuries never show up in a box score.

It ranks risk; it can’t say why any one player got hurt, and it isn’t medical advice. College results appear only as totals. Not affiliated with the NBA or the NCAA.
